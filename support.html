How to play

Invaders in seven colours fall from the top of the screen. Tap the Red, Green or Blue button to fire a circle of that colour. Press several buttons together to mix colours: red + green = yellow, green + blue = cyan, red + blue = magenta, and all three = white.

A circle only destroys the first invader of its own colour that it touches, and passes through the others. Only one circle can be on screen at a time. The game ends when an invader reaches the bottom.

Each invader scores 1 point per button its colour needs: 1 for red, green or blue, 2 for yellow, cyan or magenta, and 3 for white. Every 30 invaders a new wave starts at a slower pace, but they come in pairs, then threes, and so on.

Common issues

  • A mixed colour fires as a single colour: press the buttons at the same moment. Presses count as one mix only when they land within a split second of each other.
  • Nothing happens when I press a button: only one circle can be on screen at a time. Wait for the current one to hit an invader or leave the top of the screen.
  • Game Center or Play Games won't sign in: check that you're signed in on your device (Settings → Game Center on iPhone, or the Google Play Games app on Android), then tap 🏆 Leaderboard on the start or game-over screen to try again.
  • My score isn't on the leaderboard: scores below 20 aren't submitted, and nothing is submitted until you sign in. If you chose "No thanks" when the app offered sign-in, tap 🏆 Leaderboard to sign in and turn it back on.
  • I want to stop submitting scores: disconnect RGB Invaders from Game Center or Google Play Games in your device's settings.
  • Ads came back after a tip: ad-free time is stored on the device where you tipped. It can't be moved to another device and isn't restored if you reinstall the app. If a tip was charged but ads didn't stop, email us with the date of the purchase.
  • No sound: check the speaker button in the top-right corner of the game, and your device's volume and silent switch.

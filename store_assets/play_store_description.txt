invaders, colour mixing, colour matching, puzzle, reflex, boss fights, offline.

APP NAME (max 30 chars)
-----------------------
RGB Invaders: Colour Shooter


SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 chars)
--------------------------------
Retro arcade space shooter: mix red, green and blue to blast pixel invaders!


FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
---------------------------------
Red, green and blue. That's all you need — and all you get.

RGB Invaders is a fast retro arcade shooter with a colour-mixing puzzle twist. Pixel alien invaders in seven colours rain down from space, and only the right colour can stop them. Fire an expanding ring of colour at the invaders, but a ring only destroys an invader of its own colour. Everything else it passes straight through.

It's a classic space shooter that also tests your reflexes and your brain: read the colours, mix the right shot, and fire before they land.

MIX YOUR SHOTS
Tap Red, Green or Blue to fire that colour. Press buttons together to mix colours:
• Red + Green = Yellow
• Green + Blue = Cyan
• Red + Blue = Magenta
• Red + Green + Blue = White

Only one ring can be on screen at a time, so every shot counts. Pick the wrong colour and you waste precious seconds while the invaders keep coming.

EPIC BOSS FIGHTS
Clear a wave and a huge boss invader descends, striped with eight coloured bands in a random order.
• Only the colour of its lowest band can hurt it. Hit it and that band is blasted away, exposing the next one up.
• Fire the wrong colour and the boss lurches down a row, closer to the ground.
• Strip away all eight bands for a bonus and the next wave begins.
• Every boss comes down a little faster than the last.

TURBO MODE AND COMBOS
Destroy five invaders of the same colour in a row and TURBO lights up.
• A streak counter under your score shows how close you are.
• In turbo, chain five more in a row and the whole screen explodes: every invader is destroyed and scored, plus a 25-point bonus.
• Finish that combo on a boss's lowest band and the entire boss goes down with it.

WAVES THAT BUILD
Each wave of 25 invaders speeds up as it goes. The next wave starts calm again, but the invaders arrive in pairs, then threes, fours and fives. Survive long enough and every wave starts faster than the one before.

CHASE A HIGH SCORE
Primary colours are worth 1 point, mixed colours 2, and white 3. Beating a boss or clearing the screen in turbo adds a bonus on top. If a single invader or the boss reaches the bottom, it's game over. Share your score with a card and QR code, and dare your friends to beat it.

EASY TO PICK UP, HARD TO PUT DOWN
• Simple one-hand controls: just three multi-touch colour buttons
• A colour-mixing guide on screen while you learn
• Crisp 8-bit style pixel art, retro sound effects and original music
• Play offline, no internet needed
• Short rounds, perfect for a quick casual game on a break

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
RGB Invaders is free to play with occasional ads, limited to family-friendly content. Enjoying the game? Leave a tip from the tip jar to support development and play ad-free for a month, three months or a whole year. Tips stack, and there are no subscriptions.

If you love retro arcade games, space shooters and colour puzzles, download RGB Invaders and see how long you can hold the line!
